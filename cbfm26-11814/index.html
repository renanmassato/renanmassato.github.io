Automated Workflow for Dose Acknowledgment and Regulatory Traceability in Hospital Settings

About

Occupational dose communication in large hospitals often relies on printed reports and handwritten acknowledgment, increasing workload and limiting traceability. This study implemented an integrated occupational dosimetry management workflow to process monthly reports, classify records, send individualized notifications, and track acknowledgment using unique identifiers and timestamps.

How it works

The workflow comprised two stages. Stage 1 standardized monthly spreadsheets, routed environmental and area monitors to designated staff, flagged records without valid email information, classified doses, assigned unique identifiers, and stored records as PENDING. After activation by the medical physicist, Stage 2 sent individualized HTML notifications. Records changed to SENT after dispatch and to READ after one-click confirmation generated a timestamp; duplicate-prevention logic avoided repeated notifications.

Conclusion

The integrated workflow enabled automated processing, dose-record triage, individualized notification, and centralized, traceable acknowledgment tracking in a hospital setting. With acknowledgment timestamps generated for 27.84% of eligible records and 1,895 records remaining pending, the findings indicate a need for continued institutional follow-up.
